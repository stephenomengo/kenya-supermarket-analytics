# Insights (text for the dashboard text boxes)

## Page 1 - Executive Overview
Over 24 months the chain earned **KES 78.9M** at a **21.9% gross margin**, with an average basket of **KES 703**. Revenue was flat year on year (-0.2%): the business is stable but not growing. Six urban stores make **73% of revenue**, and Westlands alone makes 16%.

## Page 2 - Trends and Seasonality
Sales follow the calendar. Weekend days earn **25% more** than weekdays, the payday window (25th to 2nd) **28% more**, and public holidays **36% more**. December is about 33% above the average month. Inflation fell from 7% to under 4% over the period, but basket size stayed near KES 700, so no link to inflation was found.

## Page 3 - Stores, Products and Promotions
The top 10 of 98 products make **48% of revenue**. Staples & Grains is the largest category (27% of revenue). Margins range from 19% (Household & Cleaning) to 24% (Personal Care). Promotions cost **KES 645k in discounts (0.8% of sales at list price)** and did not lift quantities sold: on promotion days net revenue per product was about 14% lower.

## Page 4 - Customers and Payments
**42% of revenue comes from guests** with no customer ID. Among identified customers, the 25% who are "Champions" generate **66.5% of identified revenue**, while the 27% who are "Lost" generate under 6%. M-Pesa is 52% of baskets, and basket size is about KES 700 for every payment method. Loyalty tiers do not separate high from low spenders.

## Page 5 - Forecast
The weekly forecast misses by **KES 8,130 per store-week (12.0%)**, against 11,848 (17.3%) for the "average of the last 4 weeks" baseline: **31% more accurate**. Large urban stores are forecast best (8% to 10% error). About 11% error is unavoidable from random customer numbers, so there is little room left to improve.

## Questions the dashboard answers
1. How big is the business, and is it growing?
2. Which stores, products and categories drive revenue and margin?
3. When do customers buy (weekday, payday, holiday, season)?
4. Do promotions pay off?
5. Who are the most valuable customers, and how do customers pay?
6. How much will each store sell next week, and how reliable is that forecast?
